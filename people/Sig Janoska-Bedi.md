I am a third year law student who previously completed my undergraduate (Polisci, Classics 2017) and masters of international public affairs (La Follette School 2019) at UW-Madison, as well as a masters in ancient history at Oxford (Faculty of Classics 2021). Since 2017, I’ve TA’d for a handful of courses in the Political Science Department. As an undergrad, I was a member of the honors college, ran a few RSOs, and worked for the Morgridge Center. More recently, I’ve worked as a senior software engineer for a medium-sized organization in Washington, DC. I have a range of interests across the fields of social science, literature, and computer science. My legal interests generally relate to privacy and digital freedoms, executive powers and crises, institutions, and the American bureaucracy. In the area of classics, my interests revolve around late-republican Roman political ideology and its representation in later biographical writings. If you are interested, you can read more about my classics background here: [https://canes.wisc.edu/staff/janoska-signe/](https://canes.wisc.edu/staff/janoska-signe/).
