Adversarial System. Is the best way to find the truth by paying people to debate it. Other systems like the Confucian system, the judges assume everyone is lying. Here, judges pretend to be blank slates at the start of each trial. Are paid lawyers good? Is a private bar association the best way to organize the legal profession?

Juries in Common Law systems.  In the history of our common law system, juries were created by Æthelred the Unready. Original English jury system at common law was ONLY juries. The Normans brought judges to England. This is the beginning of common law. Custom is most important basis for the common law, at first. But eventually, standardization becomes important when trade arises. (NOTE: This will be a core piece of the discussion in Institutions (North).pdf

Download Institutions (North).pdf) Blackstone describes memory and custom as very important basis for the law. But the arising of standard rules and written law calls into question the role of juries. Until the 20th century, a core focus of the common law is jury-made law. The principle of stare decisis (or Precedent) arises and dictates that you should not change the law unless there is a good reason to do so.

Marion Barry. Was he let off because he was black, or because he was “Our Guy” and because the jury was hostile to federal interference with DC. That is, Maybe could it have been political as well as racial? Maybe this should be a place-specific argument. DC in particular is unrepresented in the federal government and over-policed. So this is a unique case where because of the context of the specific locality, it is justified. If there is a place in America where you can argue that the Democratic Bargain has failed, it’s Washington, DC. Baltimore, given the prevalence of police malfeasance, you could also argue for jury nullification.

How has Jury Nullification been used? By and large in America, jury nullification has not been a force for liberty. Instead, it’s been a force for custom and local practice to win out. This has sometimes helped bad people, and not to promote racial justice. One of the problems might be the inability to regulate and standardize the application of jury nullification. The cultural zeitgeist tends to dominate its application. Judges can set aside a jury verdict. This is called judgement notwithstanding the verdict. But regulating the jury takes away an important vestige of liberty. Ultimately, jury decisions represent an irreducible exercise of human judgement by citizens.

Democracy. What we’re dealing with is a version of the social contract, called the democratic bargain: we treat laws as legitimate because we can participate in the making and changing them.
