#### Pre-Work
Download Obsidian (https://obsidian.md/) to your desktop. It's free and open-source, and it's the tool I will be using to demonstrate knowledge management practices during class. Play around with it a little before class and, if it's not for you, you can delete right after class.

I like Obsidian because of its flexibility, how easy it is to personalize to your needs, and how it enables complexity of thinking in ways that other tools cannot.

The application has an active community forum (https://forum.obsidian.md/) and a number of commentators like Nicole van der Hoeven (https://www.youtube.com/@nicolevdh), who can help tailor the app to your needs and troubleshoot issues you might experience.

#### Discussion Questions
Today's goals are to get you thinking about how you work with knowledge, in its various forms. What are your goals when taking notes? Is it to learn while you type/write? Is it to structure knowledge for future reference? Is it to get good grades? How does/should your specific goals in taking notes factor into how you structure them? If you are unsatisfied with your approach to notes, would it be worth reappraising your goals?
